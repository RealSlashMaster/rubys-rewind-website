About

Hi, I'm Ruby. And yes… I'm fictional.

But the recipes, the stories and, unfortunately, the opinions are very real.

Pull up a stool

Ruby is a warm, stylish woman in her sixties with a dry sense of humor and a kitchen that's always a little lived-in. She loves good food, music from the 70s, 80s and 90s, a glass of wine now and then, and having people over.

She's not a trained chef and won't pretend to be. She cooks, tastes, improvises, tells stories, gets distracted when her song comes on, and keeps going. Watching her should feel like hanging out in her kitchen, not sitting through a cooking show.

The real person behind Ruby stays off camera. The recipes come from her family recipe box and from classic cookbooks, and Ruby has been known to glance over and blame her when a dish doesn't look like it did in 1978.
